# Reports / Loyalty, Inventory reports, Staff discipline: differences from the web

One line per difference: what the web does, what Flutter does, why. Rows marked "question" port
the web's behaviour as it is and ask the owner whether it is intended.

| Row | Web | Flutter | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| REP-LOY-001, REP-INV-001, REP-STF-002 gate | The routes have no guard; the page renders while the person's capabilities load (its cap-gated reads disabled: zeros, "Nothing recorded…", REP-ALL-030), then `<Restricted>` with `reports.noAccess`. | The three routes carry no `caps` (the page's own `<Restricted>` with `reports.noAccess` answers, as Operations does); while the capabilities load the page shows nothing, or the shell's access error with Retry. | The shell's rule (divergence SH-10): never flash zeros or empty words that are not the answer. |
| REP-LOY-015 tooltip | The tooltip header is the raw `week` (an ISO instant, "2026-09-05T21:00:00Z") and its row is labelled with the raw series key "outstanding" (English in Arabic). | The header is `fmtDate(week)` (the axis label) and the row reads "<n> orders" / "<n> points" with the plural unit only. Pinned by "REP-LOY-014/015 the card and the weekly bars". | Raw data and English in the Arabic UI (REP-ALL-024). |
| REP-LOY-015 motion | The liability bars animate even under reduced motion. | They draw at once under `MediaQuery.disableAnimations`. | Hard rule (SPEC 2). |
| REP-LOY-015 chart (kit) | Recharts bars, LTR plot, a number axis that goes below zero for a week that redeemed more than it earned; overlapping x labels are skipped (`preserveEnd`). | A local `LiabilityChart` (fl_chart) does the same: LTR in both languages, negative bars, nice whole steps, labels thinned keeping the last. | The kit's `DashBarChart` starts at zero and mirrors in Arabic (kit candidate). |
| REP-LOY-018 bar label | Each campaign's progress bar is named by the raw code ("winback", English in Arabic). | Named by the campaign's words ("Win-back" / "استعادة العملاء"). Pinned by "REP-LOY-018 (divergence) …". | English in the Arabic UI. |
| REP-LOY-009, 011, 016, REP-STF-006 error words | `<ErrorState onRetry>` with no message: "Couldn't load this" and Retry only. | Ported as is (no server message on these). | Product choice; the inventory tables (REP-INV-012) do show the server's words, as on the web. |
| REP-STF-011 failed refresh | A background refresh (Refresh, the focus refetch) that fails while the report is on screen toasts the server's words (`dawam-refresh-429` / `-failed`) AND swaps the cards for the ErrorState (the page checks `isError`). | The toast, once per kind (a new one replaces the live one), and the cards stay; the ErrorState shows only for a read that failed with nothing to show. Pinned by "REP-STF-011 (divergence) …". | The web's own intent (`dawam/live.ts` `failedEmpty`, `data/api/query.ts`: "the page keeps showing that data"); this page alone checks `isError`. |
| REP-STF-004 Refresh scope | Refetches the mounted queries under `/staff` (segment-bounded) and spins while any of them is fetching, the shell's Dawam set-up reads included. | Invalidates every read under `/staff/` through the realtime bus (`/staff-pool` untouched; a read nobody shows refetches when it is next shown) and spins while this page's report is fetching. | The realtime bus is the shared way to reach reads by path; the spinner follows the page's own read. |
| REP-STF-005 focus | `refetchOnWindowFocus` after 30 s (`visibilitychange`). | The app resuming (`AppLifecycleListener.onResume`) after 30 s, as the team area does. | The platform's equivalent of a tab becoming visible. |
| REP-LOY-004…008 percent (kit) | `fmtPercent` (Intl, `ar-EG` with Latin digits) in the KPI cards. | The KPI cards format through the kit's `DashKitFormats.percent` ("95.9%"), which drops the left-to-right marks `DashFormat.fmtPercent` puts around "%" in Arabic; the campaign rows use `fmtPercent`. Same look. | Kit behaviour, not forked; reported. |
| REP-LOY-012 bar height (kit) | The campaign bars are `h-2` (8 px). | The kit's `DashProgressBar` (6 px). | Kit behaviour, not forked. |
