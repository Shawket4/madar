# Reports / Basira (`/basira`): differences from the web

One line per difference: what the web does, what Flutter does, why. Rows marked "question" port
the web's behaviour as it is and ask the owner whether it is intended.

| Row | Web | Flutter | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| REP-BAS-008 menu side | The ⋯ button is pinned to the physical right in both languages, while the title leaves its room on the end side: in Arabic the button sits over the start of the title. | The button sits on the end side (left in Arabic), in the room the title leaves. Pinned by "REP-BAS-008 (divergence) …". | The overlap is a layout bug (the inventory flags it: port or flag). |
| REP-BAS-009 late answers | Opening a conversation and then another (or New conversation) lets whichever read answers last fill the transcript, even under the wrong title or in a new conversation; the skeleton stays until that read ends. | Only the latest open lands; New conversation drops a read in flight and its skeleton. | Race in the web (SPEC 6.3). |
| REP-BAS-018 no terminal frame | A stream that closes without an `answer` or `error` frame leaves the working line spinning forever. | The exchange shows the failure box with `basira.failed` "That question could not be answered" (a web key the web never shows) and Retry. Pinned by "REP-BAS-018 (divergence) …". | A spinner that never clears (SPEC 6.3). |
| REP-BAS-018 status words | "Request failed (<status>)" is hard-coded English in both languages. | Supplement `basira.requestFailed` with Arabic; shown only for an HTTP refusal with no `{error}` body, as on the web. | English in the Arabic UI. |
| REP-BAS-021 period | The period is `period_from.slice(0,10) → period_to.slice(0,10)` of UTC instants: September in Cairo reads "2026-08-31 → 2026-09-30". | The two instants as the org's calendar days ("2026-09-01 → 2026-09-30"), in the active timezone. Pinned by "REP-BAS-021 the provenance …". | Off by one day (SPEC 6.3). |
| REP-BAS-021 "by" | "by " is hard-coded English; in Arabic the code-like parts and the Arabic words run into each other. | Supplement `basira.provenanceBy` "by {{dims}}" / "حسب {{dims}}"; in Arabic each code-like part keeps its own direction (first-strong isolate). Pinned by "REP-BAS-021 (divergence) Arabic …". | English in the Arabic UI; unreadable bidi. |
| REP-BAS-024 minutes | Units "m"/"h" are English in both languages, and hours and minutes are rounded apart (119.6 min is "1h 60m"). | Supplement `basira.minutesShort`/`hoursShort`/`hoursMinutesShort` ("42 د", as `fmtElapsedMs` words minutes), rounded once (119.6 min is "2h"). Pinned by the two "REP-BAS-024 (divergence)" tests. | English in the Arabic UI; a rounding bug. |
| REP-BAS-004/007/014/018/031 direction | The question, the answer, a failure's words, a conversation title and a block title follow the page's direction: an English question on the Arabic page reads "?What sold best last month". | Each keeps its own direction (first-strong isolate), still aligned to the page's start. | Wrong direction (SPEC 6.2 defects). |
| REP-BAS-023, 031 motion | Basira's charts and the exchange fade-in ignore reduced motion. | Both honour `MediaQuery.disableAnimations` (charts draw at once, exchanges appear at once). | Hard rule (SPEC 2). |
| REP-BAS-023 pie / donut (kit) | Recharts: a hover tooltip "name: value" and a legend of names. | The kit donut: its legend lists each slice with its formatted value (no hover tooltip on the slices). | Kit behaviour, not forked; the figure is always visible instead of on hover. |
| REP-BAS-013 touch | "Touch" is `'ontouchstart' in window` (an iPad with a keyboard adds a new line on Enter). | Touch is Android / iOS; elsewhere Enter from a keyboard sends and Shift+Enter adds a line. | The platform's own signal. |
| REP-BAS-026 phone | The rename dialog is a centred dialog on a phone too. | A full-screen sheet below 760 (`showDashDialog`). | SPEC 6.1 (forms as full-screen sheets on a phone). |
| REP-BAS-032 question: heatmap direction | The heatmap is a plain table outside the LTR chart frame: in Arabic its row labels are on the right and the hours run right to left. | Ported as is. | REP-ALL-020 keeps plot areas LTR; unsure whether the heatmap was meant to follow. Owner question. |

| REP-BAS-007 rail title direction | The rail title is laid out in the page's direction. | The title takes its own direction (first strong letter) and lines up with the page's side, so a cut English title in the Arabic rail ends in its ellipsis. | Bidi defect (SPEC 6.3). |
